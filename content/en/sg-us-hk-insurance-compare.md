## 1. Three markets, three signals

Offshore insurance isn't a unified market. Hong Kong, the US, Singapore each send different "signals" — corresponding to different crowds, different wealth scales, different life needs. This piece puts them on one chart to help you see your position.

> Understanding the differences among the three markets matters far more than blindly believing any one market's promotion.

## 2. Three-market sketch

**Hong Kong — universal home ground**
- Mostly multi-currency savings-dividend insurance
- Friendly threshold, rich functions (split / insured change / multi-currency)
- Suits middle-class to newly wealthy, the first stop for starting offshore allocation

**US — apex inheritance**
- Mostly IUL (indexed universal life)
- Tied to US stocks, floor guarantee no loss, large leverage room
- Suits those with net assets USD 5M+, with US ties

**Singapore — steady supplement**
- SGD-denominated, extremely strict regulation, political security
- Rising threshold, fewer product choices
- Suits those already with global layout, needing SGD safe haven

## 3. A comparison table

| Dimension | Hong Kong dividend | US IUL | Singapore insurance |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|---------------------|
| Best crowd | Middle-class/newly wealthy | Ultra-high-net-worth | Global-layout holders |
| Denomination | Multi-currency | USD | Mostly SGD |
| Threshold | Low–mid | High | Mid–high |
| Function richness | High | Mid | Mid |
| Ease of start | Low | High | Mid |

## 4. Repatriated to one line

The three markets aren't "pick the champion," but **used in tiers**: Hong Kong as the base, the US for apex inheritance, Singapore for currency supplement. But for the vast majority of families, **Hong Kong is the "main account" covering 80% of needs** — low enough threshold, rich enough functions, fast enough to start.

We discuss the US, discuss Singapore, not to urge you to buy all, but to let you know: you choose Hong Kong because it truly matches your scale, not because "there's no choice."

> True allocation freedom is, after seeing all options, firmly choosing the one that best fits you.

## 5. In closing

The world of offshore insurance is far more complex than "Hong Kong vs US." Understanding the three markets' positioning, you neither undervalue yourself (feeling you must go to the US to be advanced) nor blindly follow trends (going wherever is hot). Hong Kong as main force, others as supplement, is the steadiest pace for most families.
