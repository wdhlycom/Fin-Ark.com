## 1. A year of questions, highly concentrated

Over the year we've talked with hundreds, even thousands, of families. Looking back, the questions everyone asks really boil down to a few categories. Laying them out is more useful than any macro forecast — because real demand hides inside the questions.

## 2. The three most-asked questions

- **"I have onshore policies / annuities — do I still need HK insurance?"** This is the most frequent. Our answer is always: need or not depends on whether your goal has that "USD + cross-generational + offshore" layer. Onshore policies cover onshore; offshore policies cover offshore. They don't conflict — they complement.
- **"With rates / the exchange rate like this, shouldn't I just wait?"** The second most frequent. Our view: obsessing over "the best timing" often leads to "never moving." Allocation is long-term discipline, not a market-timing game.
- **"Is HK insurance a scam?"** The third. We never dodge it: the industry has its chaos, regulation has its risks. But conflating "a technical tool" with "individual violations" is unfair to families doing serious planning. See the structure; make your own judgment.

## 3. What we learned

The deepest lesson: most people lack not information, but **a perspective that strings fragments into structure**. The flood of articles, short videos, group messages — it makes people more anxious, not less. What we can do is make the complex simple, and honest.

## 4. To next year's self

Keep doing that plain thing: don't be led by either extreme's emotion; stand on the side of the family's real scale and needs; explain every allocation decision clearly and plainly.

> We don't make the decision for you — we only make sure that when you decide, you see the whole picture, not fragments.
