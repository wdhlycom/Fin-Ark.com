> Justin Sun taught us one thing: never place your physical self within the firing range of your wealth.

He is Justin, Justin Sun.

In the simplified-Chinese internet and the eyes of many ordinary investors, Justin Sun is a complete showman, a master of ripping off retail, or a mocked marketing fanatic. But to grasp the highest logic of how global capital flows through gray zones and rule edges, you must strip away his garish entertainment packaging and stare directly at that extremely cold, clear, and precise **survival-defense art.**

Master Nao, discussing the survival strategy in "The Sovereign Individual," said a piercing line: **the first rule of wealth preservation is to never place your physical self within the firing range of your wealth.** Justin Sun is the perfect flesh-and-blood practitioner of that line. Today we won't discuss his morality; we'll only dissect that digital-loach survival art that no system can quite get its mouth around.

🔑 **1. The principle of the wealth firing range: how power surgically removes an individual**

Why are the vast majority of mainland self-made tycoons, even industry bosses, so fragile when the environment shifts?

Because their wealth and their physical selves sit within the firing range of one single, absolute-power jurisdiction. Once some industrial policy turns, or the macro needs a restructuring of stock wealth, the system only needs to apply a little friction to his physical person — exit bans, detention, consumption restrictions — and his companies, properties, and bank deposits freeze instantly.

In single-sovereign logic, **locking the person equals locking the money.**

Justin Sun, extremely early — even before the famous September-4 crypto storm of 2017 — smelled the danger of this firing range. On the eve of the system pulling the trigger, he completed a thrilling physical crossing, and from then never let himself return to any physical space where an administration could hit a one-click reset.

🌏 **2. The digital loach's triple decoupling**

Justin Sun thrives in global capital markets, even amid multinational wanted-notices and investigation rumors, flaunting wealth daily — the core is that he took decoupling to an art form.

## Strategy one: internationalize the body, find a diplomatic safe harbor.

While ordinary tycoons still hesitate over whether to get a US/European green card, Justin Sun used real money to obtain Grenada citizenship, and even through diplomatic maneuvering became Grenada's Permanent Representative to the WTO (a roving ambassador with full powers). In finance this is called sovereign-identity escalation. Under the common-law and international-law framework, an identity with diplomatic immunity means any secular state's administrative or judicial body wanting to physically arrest or search him must cross an extremely high international-diplomatic-immunity barrier. With identity, he built an injury-proof golden body around his physical self.

## Strategy two: borderless networking of assets and legal trustification.

His assets are neither mainland office towers nor US stocks, but crypto assets distributed across global decentralized public chains, offshore shell-company interests, and family trusts built by top lawyer teams in the Caribbean, Hong Kong, and Singapore. This structure has no headquarters foothold. When the system wants to freeze, it can't find a specific bank; when it wants to sue, it can't find a clear legal entity. He's like a ball of mercury drifting in the digital ocean — shatter it, and every drop is still money.

## Strategy three: offshore defense of information and public opinion.

As a finance person who reviews firsthand foreign-media intelligence daily, I found Justin Sun's marketing is never random. Every time he speaks loudly on social media, the essence is offshore-ing public attention. By binding himself as an opinion leader of the global crypto community, he reframes any potential judicial investigation, in public opinion, as traditional strong powers' suppression of a new digital civilization. This cognitive reframing bought him enormous rule-flexibility.

⚖️ **3. The cold truth on the loach's body**

Someone might think: Justin Sun is an extreme, non-typical case, drifting and rootless, with no sense of belonging; I just want to live steadily on the mainland and needn't learn from him.

This is exactly the biggest misconception of the cognitively mediocre — you needn't become him, but you must understand the disaster-prevention logic he proved at extreme cost.

In this era of maxed-out uncertainty and fiat money heading toward its inflationary endgame, if you still cling to the old agricultural mindset of "where my body is, there my money is, there my identity is," when the storm comes you won't even qualify to be a loach.

Justin Sun uses diplomatic identity and decentralized assets to fight the world's top-tier regulatory firing range; many mainland friends can, entirely through Hong Kong — that compliant, low-cost springboard — achieve a family's **primary decoupling.**

In Hong Kong, the only common-law center for Chinese people:

- Taking out a USD policy moves part of your assets out of the firing range of a single currency and single administrative jurisdiction;
- Obtaining a Hong Kong Top Talent or High Talent identity builds the family a dual firewall that is legal, compliant, and ready to cross jurisdictions to survive anytime;
- Opening an offshore brokerage or bank account finds a legal lifeboat, moored in deep water, for the stock wealth accumulated over the past twenty years.

🏛️ **4. At the edge of the system, build an exit gracefully**

Twenty years in the industry, I've seen too many big bosses who boasted tough connections and deep backgrounds in the business sea — when the juggernaut of the era's cycle rolled over, they were wiped out without surviving a single round. The human-relationship networks they superstitiously trusted were fragile to the point of uselessness against systemic extraction and restructuring.

Justin Sun's digital-loach survival art tells us: true freedom and safety never come from how good your relationship with the system is, but from **how far your distance is from the system's firing range.**

Wealth preservation is a precise calculation about boundaries and logic. We don't seek to defy the rules, but must, like the smartest offshore capital, on sunny days, learn to use Hong Kong — that most precious rule-isolation frost of the Chinese world — to complete the physical separation of assets from soft spots.

When the wall grows ever higher, when the storm's thunder rumbles faintly from afar, every clear-eyed preserver should ask themselves: **if the wind shifts tomorrow, are my whole family and all our wealth still within the same firing range?**
